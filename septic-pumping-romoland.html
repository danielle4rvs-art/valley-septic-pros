Septic Service in Romoland

Romoland sits along Highway 74 between Menifee and Homeland. Many of its older homes and rural lots still rely on septic systems, some of them decades old.

Older systems benefit most from regular pumping and a careful look at baffles, lids and the drain field.

Good to know for Romoland homeowners

  • Older homes should have the tank checked at every pumping
  • Avoid flushing wipes, grease and harsh chemicals
  • Call early if toilets gurgle or drains slow down

Romoland septic questions

Is my Romoland home on septic or sewer?

It depends on the property. Older homes and rural lots are often on septic. Your property records or a technician visit can confirm it.

How often should I pump?

Every 3 to 5 years for most households.

Can you help if I'm selling my Romoland home?

Yes. A septic inspection gives buyers and agents a clear picture of the system before escrow.
