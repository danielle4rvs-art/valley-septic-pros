Pumping and maintenance

How often should my septic tank be pumped?

Every 3 to 5 years for most homes, depending on household size and water use. Systems with pumps or alarms should be checked more often.

How much does septic pumping cost?

It depends on tank size, how full it is and how easy the lid is to reach. You get a free quote by phone and approve the price before work starts.

Do I need to be home for a pumping?

Not always for routine pumping. If there's an active problem, it helps to be there so the drains can be tested afterward.

Will there be a smell during or after pumping?

Some odor during and right after service is normal. It usually clears outside within a few hours.

Is it okay to use a garbage disposal with septic?

Yes, but use it lightly. Food waste fills the tank faster, so plan on more frequent pumping.

What should never go down the drain?

Wipes (even flushable ones), grease, paint, chemicals and harsh cleaners. Only human waste and toilet paper belong in the toilet.

Problems and repairs

How do I know if I need septic repair?

Slow drains, gurgling pipes, sewage odors, standing water or soggy patches over the tank or drain field, and unusually green grass in that area.

What causes septic systems to fail?

Skipped pumping, too much water at once, tree roots, a compacted or saturated drain field, and flushing things that don't break down.

How long does a septic repair take?

Small repairs like lids, baffles and filters are often done the same day. Larger drain field work can take several days plus permit time.

Does homeowners insurance cover septic problems?

Usually not for normal wear or failure, though sudden accidental damage may qualify. Check your policy.

Inspections and new systems

What's the difference between pumping and an inspection?

Pumping empties the tank. An inspection checks the tank structure, baffles, levels and drain field and reports on their condition.

Do I need a permit for septic work?

New systems, replacements and many larger repairs need a Riverside County permit. The technician will tell you what applies.

Can I add a bedroom or ADU on septic?

Sometimes, but the system may need to be evaluated or enlarged, and county approval is usually required.
